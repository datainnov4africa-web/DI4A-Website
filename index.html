About Data Innovation for Africa

Data Innovation for Africa (DI4A) is an initiative of the African Development Bank's Statistics Department. It helps national statistical offices, central banks and public institutions harness new data sources — satellite imagery, big data, artificial intelligence — to produce timelier, more granular statistics for policy.

This site shares the training material produced by the DI4A team: reproducible notebooks, presentations and step-by-step guides. Everything is free to reuse with attribution.

  • Reproducible

    Every lab runs end-to-end from a single parameter, in the cloud or on your laptop.

  • Bilingual

    Materials are provided in English and French whenever possible.

  • Open

    Content under CC BY 4.0, code under MIT. Cite "Source: AfDB – DI4A".
