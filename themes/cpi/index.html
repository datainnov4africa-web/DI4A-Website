Theme

Price Statistics & Alternative Data for the CPI

The consumer price index guides monetary policy, indexes wages and deflates the national accounts: a one-point error is paid for everywhere. This theme covers the CPI from definition to compilation, the COICOP 2018 classification that structures the basket, and a hands-on lab that turns online and administered prices into an experimental index for Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal.
